## Part 1: The Scavenger's Reality (Chapters 1-9)

### Chapter 1: The Red Moon Awakening
*   **Plot:** Rudra awakens naked and freezing in a massive subterranean sewer.
*   **Conflict:** Encounters a Slime (acidic scavenger).
*   **Resolution:** Analyzes the Slime’s core as a weakness. Sacrifices his arm to punch through the acid and rip out the core. The Slime dissolves.
*   **Key Moment:** Rudra instinctively eats the core (Rank 10 Anchor event). It heals him slightly and stabilizes his body.
*   **World Building:** He exits the sewer to see two moons (one white, one red/broken). Realizes he is not on Earth.

### Chapter 2: The Scavenger’s Reality
*   **Plot:** Rudra struggles against severe hypothermia in a massive junkyard.
*   **Action:** Scavenges a heavy canvas sack for warmth and a single steel-toed boot.
*   **Encounter:** Hides from a "Wretch" (blind, pale humanoid). Realizes the Wretch ignores him because he has zero caloric value.
*   **Theme:** "First you survive. Then you matter."

### Chapter 3: The Wall & The Cost
*   **Plot:** Rudra attempts to enter the walled city of Hull.
*   **Obstacle:** Guards demand an entry fee (2 Silver Stags) or identification.
*   **Strategy:** Feigns being a mute beggar to avoid revealing his alien origin/language deficit.
*   **Result:** Failure. He is pushed into the mud. Watches a rich merchant enter.
*   **Lesson:** "Suffering is not currency."

### Chapter 4: The First Week (Montage)
*   **Time Jump:** Covers Days 3-15.
*   **Development:**
    *   Builds a lean-to shelter from scrap.
    *   Adapts to eating "Rat-Folk" flora (micro-dosing poison).
    *   Learns the language by eavesdropping at the gate.
    *   **Physiology:** The mana in the air and the core he ate are mutating him, making him denser and improving night vision.

### Chapter 5: The Tear
*   **Event:** A "Stage 1 Tear" (dimensional rift) opens near the junkyard.
*   **Monsters:** "Void-Dogs" (chitinous hounds) spill out.
*   **Observation:** Rudra watches High-Rank Ascenders (a Fire Mage and a Hammer Warrior) slaughter the beasts.
*   **Motivation:** He stops just wanting survival; he starts craving *Power*.

### Chapter 6: The Stray
*   **Plot:** A wounded Void-Dog separates from the pack and targets Rudra.
*   **Combat:** Rudra uses his arm as bait (again) to lure it in, then stabs it with a sharpened rebar.
*   **Introduction:** **Kael Vance** (Rank 9 Ascender) appears. He is impressed by Rudra's ruthlessness.
*   **Interaction:** Kael harvests the core and tosses it to Rudra as a reward/pity.

### Chapter 7: The Consumption
*   **Dilemma:** Sell the core for entry to the city (safety) or eat it (power)?
*   **Choice:** Rudra eats the Void-Dog core.
*   **Effect:** Rapid healing of his mangled arm. Muscle density increases. The "hunger" in his chest is temporarily sated.

### Chapter 8: The Breakdown
*   **Conflict:** A Rat-Folk (Muridae) scavenger steals Rudra’s stash of copper and threatens him.
*   **Climax:** Rudra kills the Rat in cold blood using his new strength.
*   **Aftermath:** Emotional breakdown. He realizes he has crossed a moral line. He is no longer a civilian; he is a killer.

### Chapter 9: The Introduction
*   **Plot:** Rudra needs to leave the slums due to the killing (fear of retaliation).
*   **Opportunity:** Spots Kael Vance at the market looking for porters for a suicide mission through the "Red Zone."
*   **Action:** Rudra volunteers, demonstrating his strength by lifting a heavy ammo crate.
*   **Result:** Hired for 5 Silvers and a Letter of Passage.

## Future Plot Points / Outline Ideas

### Immediate Arc: The Red Zone Convoy
*   **The Route:** The convoy must pass through a "Red Zone" (High Abyssal Activity area).
*   **The Threat:** Expected ambush by higher-tier monsters (Stage 2 or 3) or bandits.
*   **Rudra's Role:** He is "luggage" until things go wrong. He will likely need to step up when the Ascenders are overwhelmed or distracted.
*   **Development:** Rudra needs to acquire actual gear (maybe looting a dead guard/porter) and perhaps his first proper weapon.
*   **Interaction:** Deepening the dynamic with Kael. Kael realizing Rudra isn't just a "strong mute peasant."

### Long Term Goals
*   **The City:** Reaching the destination (Valoria? Ferrum? Another city?).
*   **Registration:** Rudra needs to officially register with the Guild to stop being invisible/illegal.
*   **The Magic System:** Rudra needs to understand *what* he is. He is eating cores raw (The Path of the Beast/Monster) instead of using Alchemy. This makes him dangerous and potentially "Twisted" in the eyes of the Guild.
*   **Earth Connection:** Discovering other Anchors or hints of Earth's influence (e.g., seeing a "Fordism" factory or "Tesla" coil).
