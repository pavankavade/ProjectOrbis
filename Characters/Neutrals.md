# **NEUTRALS OF ORBIS**

### **1. The Guild Manager: Overseer Aldous Graves**
**"The Bean-Counter of Hull"**

**Role:** Branch Manager of the Ascenders Guild in Hull (Province of Brine). He is the party’s primary quest-giver and immediate bureaucratic hurdle.

**Background:** A Rank 5 (Iron) Ascender from the Guild's "Ledger" division. He permanently injured his casting arm years ago and traded field work for a desk.

**The Archetype (The Corporate Sociopath):** Graves views Ascenders not as heroes, but as depreciating corporate assets. He speaks entirely in the language of risk-assessment, ROI, and mortality rates. If a village is attacked by monsters, he won't send an elite Silver Rank team because the village's tax output doesn't justify the deployment cost; he'll send cheap Copper Ranks (like Rudra) because they are expendable.

**Story Function:** He represents the cold, uncaring nature of the Guild's machine. The party constantly has to outsmart his red tape, forge documents, or bribe him with rare cores to get the access or gear they need.

**Key Quote:** "I’m not paying out widow-pensions for a Rank 9 squad. If you can't close the Tear with the cheap ammunition, do the Guild a favor and die inside the rift so I don't have to process your retrieval paperwork."

### **2. The Regional Lord: Baron Caspian Locke**
**"The Admiral of the Iron Coast"**

**Role:** The Baron of Brine (The Eastern Coast), one of the five rulers sitting on Valoria's Iron Council.

**Background:** Locke controls the naval hubs, the shipyards of Hull, and the eastern smuggling routes. He is an aging, weather-beaten military aristocrat missing his left eye.

**The Archetype (The Pragmatic Warlord):** Unlike the golden, performative nobles of Aurelia, Locke is a gritty survivor. He maintains his grip on the province through a brilliant, unbreakable network of coastal and hill fortresses designed for brutal wars of attrition. He is paranoid, incredibly wealthy, and plays a dangerous political game against the other Barons.

**Story Function:** He is a "Grey" ally. He might hire the party to secretly assassinate an Abyssal cultist inside his city, not because the cult is evil, but because they are disrupting his shipyard labor quotas. He provides high-level political cover for the party, but only as long as they are useful. Isolde deeply respects him; Kael heavily distrusts him.
