
# **CHARACTER SHEET: KAEL VANCE**

**"The Runaway Shadow"**

> **Design Note:** This character is heavily inspired by the "Reluctant Assassin Heir" archetype (e.g., Killua Zoldyck), adapted to fit the darker, industrial fantasy setting of Orbis.

### **I. VITALS**

* **Full Name:** **Kaelen "Kael" Vance** (He despises the surname).
* **Age:** 20.
* **Origin:** **Nyxia** (The Twilight Realm).
* **Background:** The runaway heir of **House Vance**, the premier assassination family of the Nyxian Aristocracy.
* **Current Status:** Hiding in plain sight as a "nobody" mercenary in Valoria.
* **Rank:** Rank 9 (Copper).
* **Power Assessment:** Kael is **not** strong enough to fight a Rank 6. At his best, he can handle multiple enemies of the same rank (Rank 9) or a single enemy one rank above (Rank 8). Attempting to fight a Rank 6 is suicide.

---

### **II. THE BACKSTORY**

* **The Family Business:** House Vance doesn't just hire out spies; they breed "Living Weapons" for the High Council of Nyxia. Kael was raised in a windowless fortress where "training" involved exposure to poisons, electro-stimulation, and sleep deprivation to build immunity.
* **The Breaking Point:** At age 16, he was ordered to assassinate a target he respected (perhaps a kinder sibling or a teacher). Instead of completing the mission, he killed his handlers, disabled the family’s tracking wards, and fled south to Valoria.
* **The "Desk Job" Dream:** Kael seeks **boredom**. His childhood was *too* exciting. He claims he wants to be a "Rank 6 Bank Guard" because he genuinely craves a life where nobody tries to kill him before breakfast.
* **Why Valoria?** It's a meritocracy. In Valoria, if you can fight, nobody asks about your past. It’s the perfect place to disappear.

### **IV. PERSONALITY & QUIRKS**

* **The "Cat" Demeanor:** He is fickle, aloof, and observantly quiet. He sits on high places (roofs, crate stacks) rather than chairs.
* **Sweet Tooth:** He has a vice. In this gritty industrial world, he is obsessed with **"Sugar-Glass"** (expensive, refined candy from Aurelia). He spends most of his mercenary pay on it.
* **Twisted Morality:** He has no issue with killing. To him, death is a transaction. However, he has a violent hatred for **"Manipulators"**—anyone who uses mind control or slavery (reflecting his hatred of his family).
* **Relationship to Rudra (The "Humanity" Dynamic):**
* **The Contrast:** Kael is skilled but emotionally hollow; Rudra is unskilled but driven by raw, desperate will.
* **The Attraction:** Kael is drawn to Rudra because Rudra fights with the kind of chaotic, terrifying desperation that Kael was trained to suppress. Rudra reminds him of what it’s like to actually *care* about living.
* **The Protector:** Kael acts like he doesn't care ("You're an idiot, Rudra"), but if anyone touches Rudra, Kael will silently slaughter them. He protects Rudra because Rudra represents the "normal life" Kael can never really have.



### **V. NARRATIVE HOOKS (Future Plot Points)**

1. **The Family Tracker:** In a future arc, a "Hunter" from House Vance (perhaps an older brother) shows up in Valoria. Rudra realizes the "lazy" Kael is terrified for the first time.
2. **The Power Gap:** Kael is constantly holding back. When Rudra eventually gets into a fight he can't win (maybe against a Rank 5), Kael will be forced to use his "True Assassin Techniques," revealing he is much stronger than a Rank 9, but risking blowing his cover.
3. **The Teacher:** Kael teaches Rudra not just how to fight, but how to *kill*. This creates tension, as Rudra tries to hold onto his humanity while Kael tries to strip it away for Rudra's survival.
