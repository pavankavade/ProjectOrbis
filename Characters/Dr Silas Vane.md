# **CHARACTER SHEET: DR. SILAS VANE**

**"The Grave-Touched Chirurgeon"**

**Age:** 45

**Origin:** Solace, Valoria (The hospital town dedicated to broken soldiers).

**Background:** Formerly a Rank 5 (Iron Elite) Chief Medical Officer for the Guild's Vanguard. Silas was an unparalleled genius in bio-alchemy and soul-trauma.

**Current Status:** Operating at a Rank 8 (Bronze) Output, occasionally risking Rank 7. He walks with a pronounced, agonizing limp, leaning heavily on a solid iron cane. He is a functioning addict, wandering the dregs of the mercenary world.

**Family Status:** Estranged. His wife, Elara, is a Rank 3 (Platinum) Director in Guild Operations. She took their child and left him three years ago, unable to watch his self-destructive addiction consume him.

### **I. THE FALL (The Rank 2 Tear)**

Four years ago, Silas’s elite medical unit was dragged into a catastrophic Stage 2 Abyssal Tear. The entity that emerged was not a mindless beast, but a sentient, Abyssal Necromancer.

The squad managed to execute the Necromancer at a massive cost, but the Tear didn't close. A second, even larger Abyssal Titan began to rip its way through the rift. Out of mana and knowing his entire squad would be slaughtered, Silas committed the ultimate heresy: he cracked the Necromancer’s skull, extracted its wild, sentient Core, and swallowed it raw to force a massive, temporary power boost to seal the Tear.

It worked. He saved his squad. But it triggered The Schism.

### **II. THE CURSE & THE ADDICTION**

The Necromancer’s soul did not dissolve; it parasitized Silas's Rank 5 Geodesic Core.
If Silas attempts to draw the dense mana required for his Rank 5 or Rank 6 boons, the Necromancer "wakes up." His shadow moves independently, he hears the dead speaking, and the monster actively tries to hijack his nervous system to cast highly illegal death magic.

To keep the entity comatose, Silas is heavily addicted to "Lethe-Root Extract," a highly toxic alchemical nerve-suppressant. He drinks it from a silver flask like water. The drug keeps his soul quiet, but it destroys his body—causing the chronic nerve pain and limp that ruined his marriage and career.

### **III. THE "BIO-ALCHEMIST" DECK**

Silas despises flashy elemental magic. He is a master of the "Path of Rites" (Alchemy). He doesn't throw fireballs; he throws weaponized biology.

### **IV. PERSONALITY & QUIRKS (The "House" Archetype)**

* **The Misanthrope:** He firmly believes that "Everybody lies, but their blood doesn't." He is rude, arrogant, entirely dismissive of anyone else's intelligence, and treats the Guild's religious "Path of Grace" as a symptom of brain damage.

* **The "Lazy" Tactician:** During a fight, Silas rarely engages physically. He leans on his cane in the backline, popping painkillers, actively criticizing the party's fighting forms. He waits until the party is nearly dead before sighing, identifying the exact biological weakness of the monster, and throwing the perfect chemical countermeasure to end the fight instantly.

* **The Ulterior Motive:** He does not travel with Rudra out of the goodness of his heart. Silas recognized instantly that Rudra can consume raw monster cores without suffering The Schism or Abyssal mutation. Silas views Rudra as the cure. If he can figure out Rudra's biological anomaly, Silas believes he can reverse-engineer it to purge the Necromancer from his own soul, get his Rank 5 power back, and win his wife and kid back.

### **V. DYNAMICS WITH THE PARTY**

**With Rudra (The Lab Rat & The Cure):**
Silas treats Rudra less like a friend and more like a priceless, infuriatingly fragile petri dish. He is constantly tricking Rudra into undergoing painful medical tests, drawing his blood, and aggressively lecturing him on how not to accidentally kill himself. Rudra hates Silas’s abrasive bedside manner, but he relies entirely on Silas's unmatched medical genius to survive.

**With Isolde (The Clinical Disappointment):**
Both are disgraced Valorian commanders. Silas views Isolde’s Valorian honor and her lingering guilt over her dead squad as a "predictable psychological defect." He constantly mocks her martyrdom, telling her that her troops died because they had weak cardiovascular systems, not because she retreated. Isolde despises him for his total lack of respect for the dead, but Silas is the only person who never pities her, which she secretly respects.

**With Kael (The Annoying Child):**
Kael thinks he is a cold, emotionless killer. Silas, a 45-year-old medical sociopath who has seen true horrors, finds this adorable. Silas constantly psychoanalyzes Kael out loud, accurately dissecting Kael's "daddy issues" and runaway noble complex right in the middle of combat, just to watch Kael lose his temper.

**With Fizz (The "Rival" Department):**
This is the ultimate Chemistry vs. Engineering rivalry. Fizz thinks all problems can be solved with perfect spatial geometry and runic engineering. Silas thinks all problems can be solved with biological manipulation and raw chemistry. They aggressively argue over the "optimal" way to kill a monster, treating the battlefield like an academic debate while the rest of the party is fighting for their lives.
