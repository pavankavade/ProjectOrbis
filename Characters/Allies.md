# **ALLIES OF ORBIS**

### **1. The Guild Receptionist: Mira Kovo**
**"The Paper Shield"**

**Role:** Senior Desk Clerk at the Hull Guild Branch. She stands between the party and Overseer Graves.

**Background:** A former Rank 8 Scout who retired after losing her team in a "routine" clear. She knows exactly how dangerous the Guild's bureaucracy is.

**The Archetype (The Tired Big Sister):** Mira is perpetually exhausted, buried under mountains of paperwork, and fueled entirely by caffeine and spite. She secretly roots for Rudra’s "suicide squad" because they are the only ones who actually *clean up* the mess instead of just posturing. She frequently "loses" the party's infraction reports or "accidentally" stamps their high-risk bounty requests as approved before Graves can reject them.

**Key Quote:** *"If you die out there, I’m not filling out the Form 12-B Casualty Report. I’m just going to list you as 'Missing on Vacation'. It’s less paperwork. Now get out."*

### **2. The Merchant: "Uncle" Vargus**
**"The Scavenger King"**

**Role:** Black Market Fence and Traveling Merchant.

**Background:** Vargus runs "The Rolling Emporium," a massive, armor-plated wagon pulled by two grumpy Ox-Beasts. He operates in the grey zones between Guild regulation and the law.

**The Archetype (The Shrewd Enabler):** Vargus is the only merchant willing to buy the "questionable" loot Rudra pulls out of tears (like corrupted cores) or the "stolen" high-grade equipment Kael... acquires. He provides the party with essential illegal goods: uncapped healing potions, Valorian Fire-Whiskey for Isolde, and rare Aurelian Sugar-Glass for Kael. He pretends to be a greedy miser, but he often gives them "credit" when they are broke, knowing they are the only ones crazy enough to bring him the S-Tier loot.

**Key Quote:** *"Ah, a Rank 6 Drake-Tooth? Illegally harvested? Highly radioactive? ... I'll give you fifty gold. And throw in a bag of lemon drops for the scary child."*
