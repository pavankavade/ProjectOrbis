# **CHARACTER SHEET: PHINEAS "FIZZ" TINKER**

**"The Paranoid Runesmith"**

**Age:** 23

**Origin:** Ferrum (The Iron South) – The Smog Level.

**Background:** A disgraced former apprentice from the highly prestigious Kirloskar Pneumatic Forges in Ferrum. He was unceremoniously fired after miscalculating the diameter of a high-pressure containment rune, which subsequently blew the roof off the testing facility.

**Current Status:** Unlicensed Rank 8 (Bronze) Runesmith. He is currently hauling his heavy equipment through the mud while frantically studying for his Azure-Tier (AZ-900) Sigil Certification—a grueling Guild exam he must pass to legally practice high-level artifice again.

**Aesthetic:** He clanks when he walks. He wears a heavy canvas apron stuffed with silver compasses, brass protractors, vials of luminous alchemical ink, and sticks of grounding chalk. His fingers are permanently stained with soot and acid burns, and he constantly has a spare piece of chalk tucked behind his ear.

### **I. THE COMEDIC ANGLE (The "Sacred Geometry" Nerd)**

Fizz is a theoretical genius trapped in a world of practical, messy violence. While Kael and Isolde fight with pure instinct and physical prowess, Fizz believes that all magic is a delicate, highly volatile form of architecture.

He refuses to cast "wildly." Everything must be measured, drawn, and aligned perfectly with the planetary rotation. His comedy stems from his absolute, manic panic when the chaotic reality of an adventure ruins his perfectly drafted magic circles. A drop of rain, a smear of mud, or an enemy stepping on his chalk lines causes his spells to mutate spectacularly. He doesn't pray to the Gods; he complains about their "sloppy spatial alignment." He is the guy screaming about angular geometry and asking the terrifying Void-Dog to "hold still for just three seconds" while he finishes drawing a circle.

### **II. THE "GEOMETRIC BINDING" BUILD**

Fizz’s soul is not suited for raw elemental channeling. He processes magic externally, using his mana purely as the "ink" to activate physical runes and circles.

### **III. PERSONALITY & QUIRKS**

* **The Exam Panic:** Whenever the party is resting, Fizz is aggressively reading his thick, water-damaged study tomes for his AZ-900 Azure-Tier exam. He frequently tries to quiz the rest of the party on Draconic rune-syntax, which they uniformly ignore.
* **Chalk Anxiety:** He is incredibly protective of his high-grade Orichalcum chalk. If he drops a piece and it breaks, he mourns it like a dead pet.
* **The Hoarder of Junk:** He constantly picks up shattered weapons, broken monster cores, and useless scrap after battles, claiming he can "re-etch the lattice and make it useful." His bags weigh a hundred pounds, mostly filled with garbage.

### **IV. DYNAMICS WITH THE PARTY**

**With Rudra (The Anomaly):**
Because Rudra absorbs raw monster cores directly without any Alchemy or Rites, Fizz views Rudra as a walking, breathing violation of the Laws of Physics. He is simultaneously terrified of Rudra and scientifically fascinated by him. Fizz constantly tries to sneak up on Rudra while he's sleeping to measure his skull with calipers or hold a protractor up to his chest to see how the mana is circulating, which usually results in Rudra nearly punching him.

**With Isolde (The Canvas):**
Isolde is an unmovable wall of armor, and Fizz views her as the perfect blank canvas. He is constantly trying to carve unauthorized "efficiency runes" or "thermal vents" onto her heavy Piston-Maul and shield. Isolde tolerates him because his Kirloskar training means he does know how to repair her heavy gear, but she frequently has to physically pick Fizz up by his collar and move him behind her when his unstable canvas arrays start sparking.

**With Kael (The Philistine):**
Kael relies on speed, silence, and invisible wind blades. Fizz despises Kael’s fighting style because it leaves no physical magical signature to study. Kael, in turn, hates Fizz because Fizz is loud, constantly muttering mathematical equations to himself, and his spells require him to loudly slap physical scrolls onto the ground. Kael frequently threatens to use Fizz as live bait; Fizz usually responds by threatening to secretly carve a "Squeaking" rune onto the soles of Kael's boots.
